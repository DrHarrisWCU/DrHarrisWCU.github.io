What you're looking at: a swinging pendulum and an LC tank circuit obey the same equation of motion, just with different variables. As the pendulum swings from side to side, energy trades back and forth between potential energy (playing the role of a charged capacitor) and motion (playing the role of current through an inductor) — exactly how an LC circuit trades energy between its capacitor and inductor. The bob's color tracks that swing: green on the left, red on the right, gray at center, matching the same convention Falstad's simulator uses for positive, negative, and zero voltage.
