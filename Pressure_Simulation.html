Formulas and Analogy:

Water Pressure: P = ρ · g · h

Water Potential Energy: PE = m · g · (h/2)

Electrical Analogy: Voltage Potential (V) ↔ Water Height (h)

Where:

  • P = Pressure (Pascals)
  • PE = Potential Energy (Joules)
  • V = Voltage (Volts)
  • ρ (rho) = Density of water (approx. 1000 kg/m3)
  • g = Acceleration due to gravity (approx. 9.81 m/s2)
  • h = Height of water column (meters)
  • m = Mass of water (assuming a unit base area, so m = ρ · h)

*Note: This simulator uses an idealized model. Real-world pressure and electrical circuits can be affected by other factors.
